Three years ago I almost missed a ransomware dropper because it was disguised as a Windows update stub. Caught it only because the timestamp was off by a few milliseconds — something no legitimate installer does.

The small things are almost always the tell. Malware authors spend enormous effort on the payload and almost none on the wrapper. That's where you find them.
